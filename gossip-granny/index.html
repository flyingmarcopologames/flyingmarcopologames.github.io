Have you heard …?!

You are a granny on a mission: one little rumour has to reach the whole town. Go from house to house, combine gossip cards, shake the pill jar – and convince everyone. But careful: the wilder your story gets, the harder it is to believe!

A rumour, a handbag, a mission

A lovingly drawn comic card game full of humour. Every run is a new walk through town – and the rumour gets crazier with every stop.

Combine cards

Source + Detail + Proof = Eyewitness! Combine gossip cards into rumours. Belief × Impact = points.

Find the right tone

Whisper, Dramatic, Sentimental … matching tones give a bonus – and every opponent has a favourite.

💊

The pill jar

Shake it! Same colours give more Belief, colourful mixes multiply the Impact. Watch out for side effects …

Questions

Is the game free?

Yes. Gossip Granny is free to play and shows an ad now and then, never in the middle of a duel. An optional video gives you an extra heart when you run out.

Is there a tutorial?

Yes. In the village, Doris explains everything step by step in a short tutorial. You can start it again any time from “Help”.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad. An Android version is in testing and coming soon to Google Play.

Which languages are available?

English, German and French.
