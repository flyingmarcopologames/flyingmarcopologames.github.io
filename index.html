Questions

Is the game free?

Yes. The Airline Chief is free to play and shows ads. An internet connection is needed when an ad is due (every eighth game week).

How do online games work?

One player creates a game and shares the 6-character game code; up to 6 friends join with it – on iPhone or Android. Everyone plans their week, and when all are done (or the time is up), every phone computes the same week. Online play is free: two short optional videos give 30 minutes of online time and help pay for the server.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 15 or newer, and Android phones with Android 7.0 or newer.

Which languages are available?

English, German and French.
