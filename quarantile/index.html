How it plays

A tactical tile-laying game with dice. Every round you plan your crew's moves – then the ship fights back.

01 · ROLL

Roll the dice

Pick a crew member and roll their dice.

02 · ACT

Spend the pips

Move, search, fight, clean slime – or build new rooms. Expensive rooms need two dice.

03 · BUILD

Find the route

Lay a path of room tiles to the goal rooms that are already visible on the map.

04 · SURVIVE

The ship strikes back

Slime grows and spreads, aliens hatch, mutate and hunt your crew.
