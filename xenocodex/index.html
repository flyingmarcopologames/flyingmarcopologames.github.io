The story

The impact could be heard a hundred kilometres away. Within hours the military has sealed off the crash site – but nobody can open the ship.

You are Dr. Carla Dominguez, linguist. Room by room you make your way deeper into the wreck: from the outer airlock across the bridge, the living quarters and the greenhouse down to the reactor core.

Every word you understand opens the next door. And slowly you begin to understand who built this ship – and why it came here.

How it plays

No dictionary, no translator. Just you, your notes and a lot of signs.

01 · OBSERVE

Find the clues

Signs, screens, plaques and panels – every word appears in several places.

02 · COMPARE

Spot the pattern

Same glyph, different context. Work out what it must mean.

03 · DECIPHER

Lock in the meaning

Enter your guess on the wrist display. Right answers unlock grammar.

04 · OPERATE

Use the ship

Read the controls, route power, open valves – and get one room further.

Questions

Do I need to know any languages or codes?

No. Everything you need is in the ship. The first chapter teaches you how deciphering works.

Is it hard?

It's a relaxed puzzle game: no combat and no time pressure in most rooms. If you get stuck, three hint levels help you along.

Do I need an internet connection?

No account and no connection needed to play. Ads between chapters need a connection, but the game also runs offline.

Which languages are available?

English and German.

Is it free?

Yes. XENOCODEX is free to play with a short ad between chapters and optional reward videos for hints.
